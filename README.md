# JapaX

**Your practice. Your peace. Anywhere.**

A digital companion for chanting, remembrance, and reflection — built for iOS.

---

## About

JapaX is a personal practice app for anyone who uses repetitive counting as part of their daily routine — whether that's dhikr, japa, rosary, simran, or simple mindful repetition. It works across all traditions and requires no account or sign-in.

The app is built around a digital mala (prayer bead) that you swipe to count, with session tracking, streaks, and full customization.

---

## Features

**Mala Counter**
- Swipe beads on a digital mala to count
- Free chant — works without selecting anything, just open and swipe
- Immersive mode activates after a few swipes, tap to exit
- Reposition and resize the mala in edit mode

**Chant Library**
- Add any word, phrase, or text in any language or script
- Set a default count per chant
- Record or upload audio per chant
- Share chants as `.jaapmantra` files

**Customization**
- 4 bead themes: Rudraksha, Metal, Wood, Pearl
- Vertical and horizontal mala layout
- Standard counts: 11, 27, 33, 54, 99, 108 + Custom
- Dark, Light, or System appearance
- Accent color themes including Black & Gold

**Stats & Streaks**
- Daily streak tracking
- Session history with duration and speed
- Lifetime chant count

**Privacy**
- No accounts. No servers. No tracking.
- All data stays on your device.
- Optional iCloud sync through your own Apple ID.
