Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2025

Your Data Stays on Your Device

JapaX does not collect, store, or share any personal information. Everything you add to the app — chants, sessions, preferences — is saved locally on your device and is visible only to you.

We have no servers. We have no user accounts. We never see your data.

No Tracking

JapaX does not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking tools. We do not know who you are, where you are, or how you use the app.

Notifications

If you enable daily reminders, those notifications are scheduled entirely on your device. Nothing is sent to or from any server. You can turn them off at any time in your iPhone Settings.

Microphone

If you choose to record audio for a chant, the recording is stored only on your device and is never transmitted anywhere. You can delete it at any time from within the app.

Religious & Cultural Neutrality

JapaX is a universal tool for personal practice — it does not represent, promote, or favor any religion, belief system, or tradition. The app is intended as a simple aid for anyone who benefits from repetitive counting, reflection, or remembrance.

Any text or content you add is your own. The developer makes no claim about its religious significance or accuracy. This app is not a substitute for the guidance of any religious authority or teacher.
